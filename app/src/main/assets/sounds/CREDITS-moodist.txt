Ambient recordings named moodist_*.ogg come from Moodist (https://github.com/remvze/moodist, MIT-licensed project),
public/sounds/. Per the Moodist README, its sounds are licensed under either the Creative Commons Zero licence (CC0,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) or the Pixabay Content License
(https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/); the README does not say which applies to each file. Both allow use in
an app without payment or attribution. They were converted for KahaniFilm to mono Vorbis (44.1 kHz), cut to at most
40 seconds with short fades, and indexed with English and Hindi words (index.json, "licence" field on each entry).
