facemesh.onnx — MediaPipe FaceMesh (Google): 468 points of one face (lips, eyes and lids, brows, outline).

The model is Google's MediaPipe Face Mesh, released under the Apache License, Version 2.0
(https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). This copy was taken from the TensorFlow.js graph
models/facemesh.json + facemesh.bin of the npm package @vladmandic/human-models 3.0.4 (MIT licence,
Copyright (c) 2021 Vladimir Mandic) and converted for KahaniFilm to ONNX (opset 17), its PReLU activations
written out as plain operations (relu(x) - a * relu(-x)). Input float [1,192,192,3] RGB 0..1 (a face crop);
outputs: the mesh [1,1404] (x, y, z of 468 points in the crop's pixels), a face score [1,1], contours [1,266].
