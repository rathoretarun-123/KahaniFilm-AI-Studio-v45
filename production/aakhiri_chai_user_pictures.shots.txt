  2. An important object is used before being introduced: 1 thing(s) held that the story never names before: something (आरव) — they come from the manner of a line ("sword in hand"); the insert of a thing's picture introduces it when there is one
  3. A decision occurs without showing motivation: every realisation or decision line comes after a reaction or a look (thought before action)
  4. A dramatic sound has no visible reaction: 0 loud sound(s) in the script, each given a startle on every face on stage and a reaction shot a beat later (the director adds them itself)

  8. An emotional change has no believable transition: no feeling jumps to a far one within half a second (faces blend over 0.25 s, reactions wait a beat)
